## Overview

The documents describe an engineer who operates at **staff level through influence rather than
authority**, prefers **incremental, reversible change over decisive cutovers**, and treats
**teaching and enablement as core work** rather than a side activity. The consistent pattern
across a 20-year career is early adoption of a technology before it is locally mainstream,
followed by organizational diffusion of that technology to other teams.
